# Turn Order - Extra Turns

#### General Rules:

1. If an effect or condition would have a player take an extra turn, the player will become the turn player of the next turn. This does not skip the next player in turn order; after the extra turn(s) is/are finished, the natural turn player order will resume.
2. If multiple extra turns are granted in the same turn, they will apply sequentially until all extra turns are played. Then, the game will resume its normal turn player order.
   1. If multiple players are granted extra turns in the same turn, the extra turns will apply in a "first in, last out" fashion, where the most recent player to be given an extra turn will take the next turn. Any other extra turns will follow the order of the most recent extra turn given until all extra turns are played.
